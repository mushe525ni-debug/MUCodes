About MUCodes

Your Ultimate BCA Companion — a free, student-first platform built to make BCA exam preparation simpler.

Our Mission

MUCodes started as a simple idea: every BCA student should be able to find previous year papers, syllabus, notes and mock tests in one place — without paying, without signing up for newsletters, without ads every two clicks.

We've curated thousands of papers across 2022–2025 for all 6 semesters and 30+ subjects. Everything is organized by semester, year, subject and type so you can find what you need in seconds.

What We Offer

Who Built This

This is a student-led project. If you spot a mistake, or want to contribute papers or notes for your semester, please get in touch. We're always looking for contributors.

Our Promise

  • Free forever — no paywalls, no premium locks.
  • No spam emails. Sign up only if you want bookmarks.
  • Papers organized the way students actually study — by semester & year.
  • Privacy-respecting: we store minimal data, all locally.

Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Information We Collect

MUCodes is designed to work without collecting personal data. We do not use analytics trackers, advertising cookies, or third-party scripts that monitor you.

If you choose to create an account, we store:

  • Your name and email address (locally in your browser's localStorage — not on any server).
  • A hashed version of your password (also local).
  • Your bookmarks, download history and ratings (all local to your device).

2. How We Use Information

All account data is used solely to give you a personalized experience — remembering your bookmarks, showing your download history, and keeping you logged in. Nothing is shared, sold, or transmitted.

3. Data Storage & Deletion

Everything is stored in your browser's localStorage. Clearing your browser data or using "Log out" and clearing site data will permanently delete your account and history from this device. We cannot recover it.

4. Cookies

We do not use traditional cookies. We use browser localStorage for the same purpose (remembering preferences) but with no server-side tracking.

5. Third-Party Links

Our site links to PDF files and external resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

6. Children's Privacy

This site is intended for college-level students. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Any changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions? Contact us or email mucodes.official@gmail.com.
