What I collect

Nothing, in the traditional sense. No analytics, no ad trackers, no third-party scripts following you around.

If you sign up, this is stored locally in your browser:

  • A hashed version of your password

Only to make the site feel yours — remembering what you saved, what you downloaded, where you left off. Nothing leaves your device.
